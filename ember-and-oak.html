            Home / Our story

            A kitchen built around one fire

            Coastal Karnataka cooking, Coorg coffee country, and a city that loves to eat late.

            Where it started

            Our chef grew up between a Mangalorean family kitchen and a Coorg coffee estate, where the best food was cooked slowly over wood. After a decade in kitchens in Bengaluru, Mumbai and abroad, Ember & Oak is that memory with a city address: one long hearth, seasoned oak and coconut husk, and produce from farms within a day's drive.

            We cook what the morning brings. Ragi from Mandya, pepper from Coorg, fish from the Karavali coast. Nothing is rushed, including you.

            A day at the hearth

            The fire is lit once each morning and managed all day. Each stage of it cooks something different.

            1. Lighting, 9 am

              Oak and coconut husk are lit. Bread and stocks go in as the fire climbs.

            2. Full flame, noon

              The hottest hours: searing, charring corn and blistering peppers for lunch.

            3. Embers, 7 pm

              The fire settles into coals for dinner. Fish, meat and vegetables cook directly on the embers.

            4. Ashes, midnight

              Beetroot, onions and tomorrow's lamb are buried in the dying heat overnight.
