How to use
  1. Open this page on your phone and allow camera access.
  2. Slowly move the phone over the table until the dish lands on it.
  3. Walk around or bring the phone closer. The dish stays where you placed it, at real size.

Demo: the dishes are built in code to show the experience. In production each dish is a scan of the real plate (see solution.md). Steam plays in AR on Android. On iPhone the dish places correctly, but steam needs a hand-made USDZ file.
